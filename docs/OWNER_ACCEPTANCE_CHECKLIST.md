# Owner acceptance checklist

- [ ] Persian RTL and English LTR pages match the approved visual direction on
      desktop and mobile.
- [ ] All public copy, contact information, services, industries, integrations,
      and case-study claims are approved and factually real.
- [ ] Customer project, milestone, file, finance and support workflows match the
      intended operating process.
- [ ] Staff roles and permission assignments match real team responsibilities.
- [ ] Production domain, DNS, SMTP sender, TLS, privacy notice and retention
      rules are approved.
- [ ] A target-host Compose rehearsal, malware test, backup and isolated restore
      have passed and their artifacts are stored off-host.
- [ ] I understand no Supabase users or business data are migrated.
- [ ] I approve removal of the remaining legacy reference source after visual
      and feature parity review.
- [ ] I approve the recorded release commit, tag and container digests.

The more detailed operational checklist remains at
[checklists/owner-acceptance.md](checklists/owner-acceptance.md).
